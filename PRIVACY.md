# Notification Manager Chrome Extension Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Publisher: Noa Nadeem. Contact: noagoesplaces@gmail.com.

Notification Manager lets you review and organize your primary Google Calendar events and Google Tasks. This policy describes the Chrome extension and the synchronization records it shares with the optional Android companion.

## Information the extension handles

After you authorize Google access, the extension reads your primary Calendar identifier and time zone, Calendar event data, your Google task lists and Tasks, and Notification Manager records in your Google Drive app-data folder. Calendar data can include titles, descriptions, dates, times, locations, organizer and attendee information, recurrence information, event identifiers, and event links. Task data can include titles, due dates, completion status, identifiers, and notes. Calendar and Task content may contain personal or sensitive information you or other people put in those services.

The extension caches fetched items, your account identifier, preferences, dismissal records, recent-move records, queued actions, error messages, and Undo information in local Chrome extension storage. Queued and Undo actions may contain event or Task snapshots. Chrome's identity service handles Google authentication and cached authorization tokens. The extension does not ask for your Google password.

The extension uses browser tab/window information to manage its own window and open links you select. Display work-area information is used to position its window. It does not monitor your browsing history or scrape unrelated pages.

## How information is used

Information is used to display, search, filter, prioritize, move, and dismiss items; preserve preferences and offline reading; retry pending saves; provide Undo; and synchronize dismissal and recent-move history across installations connected to the same Google account.

Actions you select can change Google Calendar event times or eligible titles and Google Task dates, titles, or completion status. Calendar dismissal hides an item in Notification Manager without deleting the Calendar event; dismissal may also remove this app's priority title marker when eligible. Task dismissal marks the Task complete in Google Tasks. Inline Undo delays and background retries can cause a selected action to be saved later.

Dismissal and recent-move synchronization records can include item identifiers, item titles, original and destination timestamps, action timestamps, and restoration markers. These records are stored in your Google Drive app-data folder, which is separate from your ordinary Drive documents. The optional Android companion uses the same records to synchronize with the extension.

## Transfers and sharing

The extension communicates directly with Google's Calendar, Tasks, and Drive APIs over HTTPS. It has no developer-operated backend receiving your Calendar, Task, or Drive content. Its current code does not send that content to advertising, analytics, data-broker, or AI-training services, and does not sell user data.

Selecting an event, Task, location, support, update, or other external link opens the destination in your browser. Google, GitHub, and other destinations handle that visit under their own policies. Request access opens your configured email handler with a draft asking for the Google account email to approve. You choose whether to send it; the extension does not automatically send an email or create an access request on a server. Messages you choose to send to support are received by the publisher; do not include passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary private Calendar or Task content.

Notification Manager's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements.

## Retention and your choices

Local data remains until replaced during normal refresh/synchronization, cleared through Chrome, or removed when the extension is uninstalled. Some dismissal and recent-move records are pruned during synchronization; pruning is not an immediate deletion guarantee. Disconnect clears Chrome's cached authorization tokens and stops automatic reconnection, but does not erase all previously cached data or delete Google Calendar, Tasks, or Drive records.

You can revoke Notification Manager's Google account access in your [Google Account connections settings](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not undo earlier actions or erase existing Google data. Remove the extension or clear its local storage to remove its local data. To remove synchronized app data, use Google Drive's connected-app data controls where available or contact support for assistance. Manage your original events and Tasks in Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

Removing the extension or revoking access does not reverse Calendar or Task changes already saved. Review or cancel pending actions before uninstalling or disconnecting.

## Security and policy changes

The extension uses Chrome's extension storage and identity APIs, HTTPS connections to Google APIs, and account-scoped local records. No software or storage system can guarantee complete security. Material changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy with an updated effective date.
