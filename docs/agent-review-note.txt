PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS TO CHECK
- Each account operates on its own primary Calendar; no cross-account state/credential mixing.
- Rapid actions must not lose clicks or earlier saves; failures must remain visible/recoverable.
- Event Undo defaults to 2 seconds and accepts whole numbers 0–30; 0 hides the event immediately and starts a background save. Header Undo is independent and retains only the most recent action without a timer, including after reopening. It must safely cancel queued work or reverse a completed move/dismissal, never overwrite a later unrelated reschedule, and handle unknown outcomes/retries.
- Android app and Chrome options (timing, skin/theme, display settings) remain independent local preferences even for the same account.
- Extension actions are one centered hover/focus row: Dismiss, Pencil, Calendar, 4H, 7D, 3D, 2D, 1D. Titles clamp at 3 lines without overlapping actions; revealing controls must not shift the layout. Touch controls remain accessible.
- Recurring events can only be edited in Calendar or dismissed per occurrence. All-day spans use exclusive end dates; duration/time zones/DST and chosen-date semantics must remain correct.
- A successful action disappears or returns in chronological order when still in range; never auto-scroll or show a lingering Saved row.
- Only explicitly requested phone releases may be prepared/published.
